# Propsite24 – Haven Heights Property Page

## Upload to GoDaddy
1. Extract this ZIP on your computer.
2. Open GoDaddy Hosting / cPanel File Manager.
3. Open the website root folder (usually `public_html`).
4. Upload all files and folders from this package.
5. Make sure `index.html` is directly inside the website root.
6. Open your domain.

## Replace property photos
Put your own images in `assets/` and update the image filenames in `index.html`.

## Update property details
Open `index.html` and replace the sample Haven Heights text, price, size, location, etc.

## Enquiry
The enquiry form opens WhatsApp to 9220889500. The phone and WhatsApp number can be changed in `index.html` and `script.js`.

Note: The supplied screenshot was used only as the visual reference. The included sample images are cropped from that screenshot; replace them with the original high-resolution property photos for the final website.
